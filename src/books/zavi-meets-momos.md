When rain sends everyone running for cover, Zavi spots a tiny white puppy stuck at the top of the playground slide — soaked, shivering, and very stuck.

Getting him down takes more courage than Zavi feels. But by the time the puppy's fur dries into a funny round poof — like a little dumpling — he already has a name: **Momos**. And Zavi has a friend for every adventure to come.
