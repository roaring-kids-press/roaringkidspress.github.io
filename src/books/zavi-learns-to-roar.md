Zavi is the smallest one in almost every room — the smallest at the table, the smallest on the bus, the smallest at the very back of the class. When the world feels too loud and too big, staying quiet feels safer than being seen.

Then one ordinary day, Zavi discovers that brave doesn't mean *not* being scared. It means finding your voice anyway — and letting out the roar that was there all along.

A gentle, funny story about courage that honors a child's feelings instead of rushing to fix them.
